Appendix

Method and definitions

How the 280 answers were collected

  • 7 plain-English questions covering Manufacturing Automation, Custom Power Apps, Power Platform Consulting, Workflow & Power Automate, ERP Integration, Power BI & Microsoft Fabric, and Copilot Studio & AI Agents across 8 Australian cities = 56 city-question pairs.
  • Each question was asked across 5 AI assistants (ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude) in October 2026 for 280 total answers.
  • 56 matching Google searches and 9 Google Maps checks were collected across the same cities.
  • All 28 pages on inturing.com.au were reviewed to check page titles, service coverage, case studies, and business settings.

How answers were scored

  • A consultancy counts as “named” once per answer, regardless of how many times its name appears in that answer.
  • Every website link referenced by an assistant was recorded (3,615 source references across 708 websites).
  • Inturing's own website (inturing.com.au) was referenced 91 times across 35 answers — the #3 most-referenced consultancy website in the entire study.
  • Historical Google AI answer counts come from DataForSEO's monthly tracking of Australian Google AI answers referencing each consultancy's website from August 2025 to September 2026.
