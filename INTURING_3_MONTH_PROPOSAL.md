## 1. Executive Summary & Context

Inturing has recently rebuilt its website (`inturing.com.au`) on **Lovable** and is already generating high-quality inbound enquiries—particularly from Australian manufacturers looking to automate operational workflows on the Microsoft Power Platform. Rather than starting over, the goal of this 3-month programme is to **build directly on top of the existing Lovable site**:

1. **Tightening positioning and messaging** so Inturing remains manufacturing-led while opening up to adjacent sectors (wholesale, distribution, civil construction, property, and mid-market operations).
2. **Conducting commercial SEO and AI keyword research** to map where real buyer demand sits.
3. **Building out missing solution, industry, and city pages** directly inside the existing Lovable codebase.
4. **Launching an Insights Hub (`/insights`) plus 3 high-converting lead magnets** (Case Study Pack, 15-Point Readiness Checklist, and Executive ROI Guide).
5. **Setting up GA4, Google Search Console, and custom event tracking early**, wired directly into **HubSpot lead capture**.
6. **Executing on-page, technical, and AI search (GEO/SEO) optimisation** across all routes.
7. **Running a standing weekly catch-up** throughout the 90-day build.

> **Key Audit Finding:** We tested **280 real buyer questions** across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude across 8 Australian cities, alongside **56 live Google searches**. Inturing is already **#1 in Australia for Manufacturing Process Automation** (named in **23 of 40** answers, and recommended **#1 sixteen times**), and its website is the **#3 most-referenced source overall (91 citations)**. However, because the homepage title says *"for Manufacturers"* and the 28-page site lacks dedicated ERP Integration, Microsoft Fabric, and adjacent-sector/city pages, Inturing gets **0 out of 200 mentions** across general Power Platform, Power Automate, ERP Integration, Power BI, and Copilot Studio searches.

## 3. Proposed 3-Month Programme Scope (Mapped to Your Brief)

Every workstream below is designed to plug directly into your existing Lovable codebase and HubSpot setup, keeping what already converts while systematically expanding Inturing's reach across organic search, AI assistants, and adjacent industries.

### Workstream 1: Positioning & Messaging Refinement (Manufacturing-Led, Open to Adjacent Sectors)
We will sharpen Inturing's core messaging across the homepage, navigation, and service headers so you never lose your #1 authority in manufacturing, while making it unmistakably clear to buyers, Google, and AI models that Inturing delivers the same operational rigour to adjacent heavy-operations sectors.
- **Core Positioning Framework:** Anchor on *"Operational AI, Power Platform & ERP Automation — Built in Manufacturing, Proven Across Wholesale, Construction, Logistics & Mid-Market Operations (50–1,000 Staff)."*
- **Homepage & Meta Overhaul:** Update the Lovable homepage title, hero sub-copy, and sector proof strips so non-manufacturing COOs and Finance Directors immediately recognise their own workflow bottlenecks (order-to-invoice, approvals, field inspections, ERP sync).
- **Written Proof Strips for Existing Logos:** Add concise, high-impact written proof blurbs beneath **Burgtec**, **Schlam** (mining/heavy equipment), **Menai Civil Contractors** (civil engineering), **AustEx**, **Liquip Victoria**, **CBTech**, and **EFS** alongside the **BBCP** and **Property Network of Australia (PNA)** case studies.

### Workstream 2: Early GA4, Google Search Console & HubSpot Event Tracking Setup (Weeks 1–2)
Before new pages and lead magnets go live, we will instrument clean, reliable analytics and attribution across the Lovable site and HubSpot so every enquiry and asset download is tracked back to its exact search, AI, or referral source.
- **GA4 & Google Search Console Configuration:** Full setup, domain verification, sitemap submission, and custom conversion event tracking across the Lovable single-page/router architecture.
- **Custom Event Tracking:** Track every high-intent interaction: 3-Minute Assessment starts and completions (`/assessment`, `/assessment/power-platform`, `/assessment/manufacturing-automation`), ROI calculator interactions, lead magnet downloads, and HubSpot meeting bookings.
- **HubSpot Lead Capture & Attribution Wiring:** Connect Lovable forms and gated lead magnets directly into HubSpot CRM with hidden UTM, landing-page, and referrer capture (including tracking referrals from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google search).

### Workstream 3: Commercial SEO & AI Keyword Research
Using our initial 15-keyword AI demand dataset (**3,418 monthly AI searches in Australia** across Power BI, Microsoft Copilot, Power Apps, Power Automate, Copilot Studio, and Microsoft Fabric) plus full Google organic keyword research, we will map where high-converting commercial intent sits.
- **Search & AI Demand Matrix:** Prioritise keywords by commercial buyer intent rather than vanity traffic—focusing on ERP integration (`MYOB Acumatica`, `SAP Ariba`, `Dynamics 365`, `Pronto`, `NetSuite`), Power Platform implementation, Power BI / Fabric reporting, and practical Copilot Studio agents.
- **Page-by-Page Keyword & Prompt Blueprint:** Assign primary and secondary search terms, buyer questions, and FAQ targets to every existing Lovable page and every new solution/industry page.

### Workstream 4: Building Out Solution, Industry & Location Pages on Lovable
We will design, write, and ship new solution and industry pages directly inside your existing Lovable site architecture, matching your current UI components, navy/steel-blue aesthetic, and interactive assessment flows:
- **New Solution Pages (Closing the 0/200 Gap):**
  1. Dedicated **ERP & System Integration** page (`MYOB Acumatica`, `SAP Ariba`, `Dynamics 365`, `SharePoint` & `Monday.com`).
  2. **Microsoft Fabric & Executive Operational Reporting** page.
  3. **Microsoft Copilot Studio & Practical AI Agents** page.
  4. **SharePoint & Approval Workflow Automation** page.
- **New Adjacent Industry Pages:**
  1. **Wholesale, Distribution & Supply Chain**
  2. **Civil Construction, Engineering & Field Services** (leveraging Menai Civil & Schlam proof)
  3. **Property & Multi-Party Operations** (leveraging the PNA `$150k/yr` savings case study)
  4. Upgraded **Public Sector & Canberra Governance** page
- **Australian Delivery Hub Pages:** Launch targeted commercial landing hubs for **Sydney (HQ)**, **Melbourne**, **Brisbane**, and **Perth** so Inturing ranks in city-specific Google searches and local AI shortlists.

### Workstream 5: Insights Hub + 3 High-Value Lead Magnets
To capture operations leaders who are researching solutions before booking a call, we will build an **Insights Hub (`/insights`)** on the Lovable site and produce three practical, HubSpot-gated lead magnets:
- **Lead Magnet 1 — Flagship Case Study Pack (PDF + Interactive Page):** *"How Australian Operations Teams Save $150,000+/Year Automating Order-to-Invoice & Backend Workflows"* — a deep-dive breakdown of the **BBCP** (`>90%` faster order processing across MYOB Acumatica, SAP Ariba & SharePoint) and **Property Network of Australia (PNA)** architectures.
- **Lead Magnet 2 — Practical Operations Checklist:** *"The 15-Point ERP & Spreadsheet Automation Readiness Checklist for COOs and Operations Managers"* — paired with your existing online assessment tools.
- **Lead Magnet 3 — Executive ROI & Implementation Guide:** *"The Mid-Market ROI Guide to Microsoft Power Platform & Copilot Studio: Realistic Costs, Timeframes, and Payback Windows in Australia."*
- **Insights Hub Architecture (`/insights`):** Built in Lovable with category filtering (Manufacturing, ERP Integration, Power Platform, AI & Copilot), embedded HubSpot capture forms, and direct CTAs to Harry's booking calendar and the 3-minute assessments.

### Workstream 6: On-Page, Technical & AI Search (GEO) Optimisation
Because Lovable sites are modern JavaScript applications, technical SEO and structured data must be configured carefully so Googlebot and AI crawlers (`GPTBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`) index every route cleanly.
- **Route-Level Metadata & Pre-Rendering Checks:** Ensure unique title tags, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, OpenGraph tags, and clean H1–H3 hierarchy across all existing and new Lovable routes.
- **Entity-Rich JSON-LD Schema & `/llms.txt`:** Replace the bare country-only schema with full `ProfessionalService`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, and `Founder` (Paul Del Gigante & Harry Cribb) structured data, plus a machine-readable `/llms.txt` file and updated `sitemap.xml`.
- **Sydney Google Business Profile & Partner Directories:** Verify Inturing's Sydney Google Business Profile, switch primary categories to *Business Management Consultant* + *Software Company*, guide review collection from named clients, and support profile setup on Microsoft AppSource and Clutch.

### Workstream 7: Weekly Catch-Up & Monthly AI/SEO Benchmark Reporting
We work as an extension of Harry and Paul's team with fast weekly execution and transparent reporting:
- **Weekly 30-Minute Catch-Up:** Standing weekly sprint call to review shipped Lovable pages, HubSpot lead flow, Search Console indexing, and upcoming priorities.
- **Monthly 280-Answer AI & SEO Re-Run:** Every 30 days, we re-run the exact same 280 AI questions and 56 Google searches to measure Inturing's gains against Eagle360, Barhead, Velrada, and Power Platform Experts.

### Scope Boundaries (Explicitly Out of Scope)
> **Per your brief, the following items are excluded from this 3-month programme:** Ongoing social media management, ongoing blog/newsletter content production beyond the Insights Hub launch and 3 core lead magnets, and media/PR outreach.
